# Northern Lights Forecast Guide

A free guide for aurora hunters, photographers and travellers who want to experience the northern lights.

## How to Predict the Northern Lights

Several factors influence aurora visibility:

- Geomagnetic activity (Kp index)
- Solar wind speed
- Interplanetary magnetic field (Bz)
- Local cloud coverage
- Light pollution
- Hours of darkness

## Free Northern Lights Forecast

Check aurora forecasting information, solar activity and local weather conditions using:

[Northern Lights Forecast](https://northernlightsforecast.live/)

## Best Places to See the Northern Lights

- Tromsø, Norway
- Abisko, Sweden
- Rovaniemi, Finland
- Levi, Finland
- Reykjavik, Iceland
- Fairbanks, Alaska

## Best Time to See the Northern Lights

September to March generally offers longer hours of darkness in northern regions, making aurora observation easier.

## Aurora Viewing Tips

- Find a dark location away from city lights.
- Check local cloud coverage.
- Monitor geomagnetic activity.
- Allow your eyes to adjust to darkness.
- Keep checking aurora conditions throughout the evening.
